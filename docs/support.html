Answers to the usual questions

  • Where is my data? On your phone, and backed up to your account so a new phone restores it. Profile → Your data exports everything as one file; Profile → Login → Delete Account deletes the account in the app. The privacy policy says exactly what is collected.
  • Form Check isn't counting. Get your whole body inside the green frame, feet included, and hold still for a moment so Morphe reads your build. Facing the camera reads knees; side-on reads depth. Video never leaves your phone.
  • Hey Morphe doesn't answer. It listens only while the app is open, and only once you turn it on in Profile.
  • A number looks wrong. Every stat comes from sets you logged. Open the session in Train and fix the set; the score follows.
  • Can I use it on Android? Not yet. iPhone, iOS 17 or later.
