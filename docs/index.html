About

Sparta didn’t sell motivation. It built discipline and let the results speak.

Morphe is the same product. Nothing invented, nothing padded, every number earned. You plan the session, log the sets, and the Morphe Score reflects what you actually did. Not a number someone made up.

The AI inside it speaks like a guardian, not a hype man. The register is laconic: short sentences, verbs first, zero filler, zero flattery. If it doesn’t know a number, it says so.

The brand takes three things from Sparta and leaves the rest: discipline, brevity, and the line that holds together. Every ancient line Morphe quotes carries where it is written down, and the app says plainly what it does not take from Sparta.

Built by Lucas Dilley, a solo founder in the Bay Area, for people who want to build the habit, not chase perfection. Small wins. Real transformation.

What it does

Plan. Log. Prove it.

Build

Your own workouts

A library of 190+ exercises, 150 ready workouts, and multi-week programs. Add your own when something’s missing.

Log

Every set, real weight

Weight, sets, reps, RPE. A rest timer in the Dynamic Island keeps you moving. Say “log 3 by 10 at 135” and it’s logged.

Form Check

The camera counts

On-device pose tracking reads your build, counts reps across twelve movement patterns, and tells you what it measured. Video never leaves the phone.

Progress

A score you earned

Streaks with honest outs, PRs from real logged sets, trends computed from your own history.

Together

The shield line

Train the same workout live with a partner. Weekly boards, challenges, and coach messaging. Opt-in, real scores only.

Yours

Your data, your call

Cloud backup, optional Apple Health sync, one-file export, and in-app account deletion. No analytics trackers, ever.
