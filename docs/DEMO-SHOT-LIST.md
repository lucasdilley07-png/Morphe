# Morphe demo video: final shot list

2026-10-12. Two videos from one shoot day. Every spoken line is yours,
from the October 9 Launch Lab deck. Nothing on screen is staged: real
screens, real sets, real numbers.

The pitch deck that embeds Video 1 on slide 4: https://claude.ai/artifact/UyXaF6BJWqxe2ezoVYAye7

## Before you roll (on set, top of the Notes copy)

- Latest build on the phone, signed in, Do Not Disturb on, battery over
  80%, brightness up.
- Profile → Make it yours → Hey Morphe on.
- Pick the movement Form Check counts best for you. Do 3 rehearsal sets
  on the actual phone, in the actual spot. If the count misses, don't film
  it live: use the recorded fallback (Video 1, shot C).
- Set today's workout so "what's next" answers with the movement you'll
  film.
- Screen Recording with the mic ON (long-press the record button in
  Control Center). Camera B: over the shoulder, 4K 30.
- One loud clap at the start of every take to sync the phone recording
  with camera B.
- Gym permission first. Nobody else's face in frame without their OK.

## Video 1: "One set, one take" (60–75 s). The master demo.

Used as the deck's slide 4, the coach-pitch demo, and the Day 0 post.

| # | Time | Shot | What happens | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 0–3 s | You, medium, phone in hand, gym behind | Say: "Hey Morphe, what's next?" The orb lights the screen | Your voice, room tone |
| B | 3–12 s | Screen recording | Morphe answers out loud with today's first movement | App audio, keep it clean |
| C | 12–40 s | Phone on a stand + camera B over the shoulder. **One take, no cut.** | The set. Form Check counts every rep on screen. The counted set gets its seal | Breathing, plates, the count |
| D | 40–50 s | Screen recording, then your face | Log the next set by voice: "log [your sets] by [your reps] at [your weight]". Use your real numbers | Your voice, Morphe's confirmation |
| E | 50–65 s | The outcome: the only polished shot (LUT, slow push-in) | The score moves, then the share card | Music can come up here |
| F | 65–70 s | End card | MORPHE wordmark + "Every number is earned." | — |

Edit rules:
- No music under A–D; the voice moments are the proof.
- Burn in captions for every spoken line.
- Cut a 16:9 master for the deck and a 9:16 for social.
- If the count missed in C and you used the fallback clip, the caption
  says "recorded set". Nothing claims to be live that wasn't.

## Video 2: "The number that can't be typed" (30–45 s). Same day.

The deck's cold open and the Lucas_Dilley post.

| # | Shot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Screen recording | Open History on a **throwaway test set** you logged with the camera (seal on). Edit its reps up by hand. The seal disappears |
| 2 | Camera B + screen | A real set, counted by the camera. The seal stays |

**Delete the throwaway set right after** so the fake number never sits in
your real history or records. A re-edit doesn't bring the seal back.
