# Morphe — Audits 29 and 30 (2026-10-01 → 2026-10-03)

The build → fresh-eyes audit → fix wave → device loop, applied to the
Spartan rebrand, the lore wave, the Form Check rebuild, the tour, and the
website. Each audit ran as independent reviewers who were given the code,
not the author's conclusions. This file records what they found, what was
fixed, and what was deliberately left.

## Audit 29 (2026-10-01) — the lore wave

Three reviewers: correctness, UI/contrast/accessibility, classics
fact-check. Headlines:

- **Fact-check:** 5 wrong and 18 loose renderings in the first draft of the
  sayings (speakers, situations, paraphrases presented as quotes). Every
  saying is now verbatim from a public-domain Loeb translation with its
  citation; `docs/SPARTAN-LORE.md` §8 keeps the corrections log.
- **Contrast:** accent ink in dark mode was still raw #2957D9 (3.1:1) — the
  lift gated on HSB brightness, which the brand blue passed. Now gated on
  luminance (`MorpheTheme.asText`). Button/chip labels follow their fill
  (`MorpheTheme.onFill`); the system accent asset was still gold.
- **Correctness:** cloud restore wrote the old Gold default back over the
  migrated palette; the migration marker moved into the profile snapshot
  (`paletteEpoch`). Lore replies hijacked ordinary prompts on bare
  substrings; now whole-phrase intents after every data handler. The
  level-up banner was overwritten by the "+XP" banner before it rendered;
  now held and flushed after the caller's turn.

## Audit 30 (2026-10-03) — everything since

Three reviewers: correctness, brand + design, honesty + accessibility.
56 findings. Fixed in commit "Audit 30" unless listed under *Left*.

### Correctness (fixed)
- Tour "Find people" landed on a Network section that doesn't exist with
  the feed flag off → routes to Contact (the username search's home).
- Swiping the Form Check review away left a dead camera → the sheet's
  `onDismiss` restarts the session.
- The welcome/tour sheet could present over the 0.6s launch beat → gated
  behind `launchSettled`.
- "Replay the guide" discarded unsaved profile drafts → goes through the
  same save/discard ask as Done.
- Tour hand-offs and the guide replay used wall-clock timers that another
  sheet could swallow → `pendingTourExit` / `pendingGuideReplay`, consumed
  by the sheets' own `onDismiss`.
- The window swipe-to-dismiss overrode the chats' follow-the-finger mode →
  skipped inside interactive scroll views.
- Form Check: cross-queue writes (`mirrorX`, `cameraPosition`) moved to
  their queues; clip failure paths clean the temp file, surface the
  Photos-permission reason, and honor interrupted-but-complete recordings;
  camera-counted sets log past the planned count (`allowExtra`), like
  voice; the review says "Done" (not "Log set") where nothing logs.
- Bio clamped at 220 in the tour; one saying shared by the Learn card and
  The Code sheet; the Gold migration settles only for a signed-in profile.

### Honesty (fixed)
- "log 3 by 10 at 135" now parses — `normalizedSetPhrase` turns "N by M"
  into "NxM" before both the chat and voice parsers.
- Tour examples show what the brain actually says; "Hey Morphe" copy says
  it must be turned on; Train Together lives under Train, not Network;
  check-in and challenges point where they are; "nine stops"; the photo and
  bio are not shown to others yet and the tour says so.
- "eleven movement patterns and times holds"; "190+ ready workouts" (the
  catalog holds 193).
- Form Check: no fatigue attribution in cues; "the camera" not "the front
  camera"; the pill names what the grade scores (range, tempo, alignment);
  the review's grade label is "SET", not "FORM".
- Demaratus 2 restored verbatim ("Because these they put on…").
- Website: nothing "reserved" (saved on this device), no creatine efficacy
  claim, TestFlight in future tense, the chosen line labeled "The line"
  with its honest attribution.
- App Store copy: exercise count, Form Check and Morphe AI bullets, the
  feed bullets flagged out while `socialFeedEnabled` is off.

### Accessibility (fixed)

  counter and angle read as single elements; cue icons hidden; RPE chips

- The helmet mark is hidden from VoiceOver everywhere (decorative).

- "Small wins. Real transformation." on the site — Lucas's App Store line.
- The site's "The line" quote is not one of the eight cited sayings; it is
  labeled as such. Lucas chose it.
- Share-card poster surfaces keep brand blue on black (3.46:1) for large
  type by design; the wordmark and small icons there are at the same
  value — one lit constant would lift all of them.
- Micro-label system drift (64 sites below 11pt, 13 tracking values) and
  off-grid spacing literals app-wide: a dedicated typography pass, not a
  fix wave.
- `.buttonStyle(.plain)` rows without a pressed state (123 sites): a shared
  `PressableRowStyle` is the fix; not done here.
- `presentationCornerRadius(28)` on the 9 older sheets that lack it.
- Numbered 01–04 ledgers (manifesto card, site rules): kept as the
  house-rules treatment.
- The shaker product on the site uses the AI bubble: placeholder merch.
