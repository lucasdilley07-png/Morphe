# Morphe — Audits 29 and 30 (2026-10-01 → 2026-10-03)

The build → fresh-eyes audit → fix wave → device loop, applied to the
Spartan rebrand, the lore wave, the Form Check rebuild, the tour, and the
website. Each audit ran as independent reviewers who were given the code,
not the author's conclusions. This file records what they found, what was
fixed, and what was deliberately left.

## Audit 29 (2026-10-01) — the lore wave

Three reviewers: correctness, UI/contrast/accessibility, classics
fact-check. Headlines:

  sayings (speakers, situations, paraphrases presented as quotes). Every

  substrings; now whole-phrase intents after every data handler. The
  level-up banner was overwritten by the "+XP" banner before it rendered;
  now held and flushed after the caller's turn.

## Technique Library wave (2026-10-04)

Lucas: "audit 500+ workouts and exercise variations from fitness experts and
coaches and add it to the discover page."

**What shipped:** 401 new exercises (library 194 → 595) and 126 new workouts
(catalog 193 → 319) across 17 training styles, four of them new (Olympic
Weightlifting, Strongman & Odd-Object, Cycling/Rowing/Swim, Barre &
Low-Impact). Authored as data in `Content/ExerciseLibrary/` and
`Content/DiscoverLibrary/v3-*.json`, validated by
`Tools/validate_exercise_library.py`, merged by `Tools/build_catalog_v2.py`.
Discover gained the Technique Library (search, style and body-area filters)
and each new exercise shows its reference with a link.

**Audit of the original 194** (`docs/TECHNIQUE-AUDIT-2026-10.json`): all read;
6 sourced findings fixed (one safety: the cable lateral raise cue), plus 9
unsupported claims, labels and a typo corrected without a source.

**Honest limits**
- The session's web-search allowance (200) ran out mid-research. Later work
  used direct page loads only; movements with no real source were dropped
  (about 60 across disciplines) rather than written unsourced.
- Not every reference was read in full. ExRx (68 entries), NSCA (14),
  British Cycling (5) and Yoga Journal (23) block direct loading or sit behind
  a login; those entries were checked against search-result summaries of the
  cited page. The in-app label is therefore "Reference", not "Verified".
- Sources below federation/certifying-body tier: Fitness Volt (5 strongman),
  Evolve MMA (9), JiuJitsu.com (3), ExpertBoxing (1), Pilatesology (11),
  Power Pilates (7), StrengthLog (4), Nuffield Health (2), The Prehab Guys
  (1), Experience Life (1), RunnersConnect (1), PowerliftingTechnique (1),
  Assault Fitness (1).
- Thin entries worth a human read: Larsen Press, PVC Pass-Through, B-Skip,
  Power Walk, Rowing Rate Ladder, Mambo, Over the Top, Pivot Turn, Wrestling
  Stance and Motion, hip/shoulder controlled rotations, Plié Pulse.
- All wording is Morphe's own; no coach is quoted or named. No new form
  diagrams: the 401 new entries are text-only.
- Link check: 320 distinct URLs, 238 load, 82 are refused to scripts by the
  site (403/429), 0 not-found.
- The original 194 audit was limited by the same search cap; a second pass in
  a fresh session is owed.

## Audit 31 (2026-10-04) — the day's work, read cold

One independent correctness reviewer over c8d335a..HEAD (Hey Morphe, daily
streak, reminders, Technique Library, lock-screen card). Nine findings, all
fixed the same day:

1. Appointment rings were added but never reconciled (a cancel on another
   device kept ringing) and sign-out left every pending notification behind →
   sync removes stale rings; sign-out clears the queue.
2. A tap install that threw (0 Hz mid-switch) reused the same engine forever →
   teardown replaces the engine whenever its input node was touched; a
   mid-capture resume retries twice before giving up.
3. Route recovery could re-arm the mic during a phone call → `interrupted`
   flag.
4. `isBargeCapture` survived an empty or cancelled capture → cleared on every
   exit.
5. Westward travel reset the daily streak → a last-open day after today holds.
6. A lock-screen log started a 0-second rest on superset/circuit entries →
   guarded.
7. The board reminder didn't come back when Reminders was switched on → added.
8. "See all N" opened the unfiltered library, and the two searches matched
   different fields → the query carries over; one shared matcher.
9. The library rebuilt its style index and sort on every keystroke, and every
   Today appearance re-queued nine notifications → cached per visit; once per
   day per profile.

Clean: the lock-screen card, the catalog merge (401 decode, 319 resolve), the
notification budget (~50 of 64), no double tap install.
