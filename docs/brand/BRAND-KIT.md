# Morphe Brand Kit — as supplied by Lucas, 2026-10-05

Source: `brand-kit-2026-10.png` (the one-sheet), `wordmark-glow.png` (the
wordmark on a smoke field), `circular-icon-source.png` (the glass-bubble
helmet now used for the Morphe AI button). This file transcribes the sheet so
it can be searched and diffed; the images are the authority.

## 01 Logo system
- **Primary wordmark** — MORPHE, uppercase, wide tracking. "Clean. Bold. Timeless."
- **App icon** — rounded-square glass tile with the helmet. "Modern. Recognizable."
- **Circular icon** — glass bubble with the helmet. "Distinctive. Versatile."

## 02 Brand character
A translucent, glowing Spartan in full armour (helmet with crest, plate
armour), electric-blue glass. Words beside him: STRENGTH · DISCIPLINE ·
INTELLIGENCE · PROGRESS · TOGETHER. Top-right: "HUMAN POTENTIAL AMPLIFIED BY AI".

## 04 Typography
- Heading: uppercase, wide tracking, clean and powerful.
- Subheading: modern, spacious, refined ("AI FITNESS REIMAGINED").
- Body: clean, readable, modern, for digital and mobile.
- Sample body copy: "Morphe combines artificial intelligence with proven
  fitness science to help you build a stronger, healthier, more disciplined
  version of yourself."

## 05 Brand keywords
AI-DRIVEN · DISCIPLINED · FUTURISTIC · PREMIUM · ATHLETIC · INTELLIGENT

## 06 UI / visual style
- App experience: "Clean. Premium. Instant recognition."
- Glassmorphism UI: "Modern. Luminous. Data-driven." (progress ring 78%,
  bars for strength / endurance / discipline / recovery)
- Icon in context: "Versatile. Bold. Iconic."

## 07 Tagline / positioning
**AI FITNESS. SPARTAN DISCIPLINE. MODERN PERFORMANCE.**
Pillars: Smarter Training (AI-powered personalization) · Stronger You
(measurable, real progress) · Higher Potential (discipline for a better tomorrow).
