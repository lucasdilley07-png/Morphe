# Launch content calendar — 14 days from TestFlight-live (2026-07-30)

Day 0 = the day the TestFlight link exists. Three accounts, one
funnel: Book_Of_Lucas (build-in-public POV) → Lucas_Dilley (personal
proof) → the app. Every post ends at the bio link. One pillar rule:
NEVER fake numbers in content — screenshots are real screens or they
don't post. The share cards / PR posters ARE the ad unit; the brand
kit (docs/brand-kit) has the assets.

Format legend: [BOL] Book_Of_Lucas · [LD] Lucas_Dilley · [HB] HBPC.
Each day = one primary post + story slices cut from it. Batch-shoot
days 1–4 in one session; run the trend-hunter weekly and swap any
hook that's beaten by a live trend.

## Week 1 — the honest-app launch arc

- **Day 0 [BOL]** "I spent 6 months building the fitness app that
  can't lie to you. Today strangers can finally install it." Screen
  recording: log a real set → watch the share card generate. Link in
  bio. Story: TestFlight install walkthrough.
- **Day 1 [LD]** Your REAL streak + Morphe Score on camera, phone in
  hand at the gym: "this number can't be typed in — every point is a
  logged set. That's the whole app." Post your actual session card.
- **Day 2 [BOL]** The villain hook: "Your fitness app is lying to
  you." 45s on fake streaks / vanity badges in the category → the
  earned-badge grid (real screen). CTA: "mine can't. Link in bio."
- **Day 3 [HB]** Comeback-series crossover: the comeback card ("a
  lapsed streak gets acknowledged once — no guilt, one ramp back
  in"). Story of missing days + what the app does about it. This is
  the emotional post of the week.
- **Day 4 [LD]** Duo Streak episode: train with a friend, both post,
  show the DUO flame appear. "Accountability is a feature now." Tag
  the friend (their consent, obviously).
- **Day 5 [BOL]** Build-in-public numbers post: screenshot the
  metrics report (real accounts, real activation %, however small).
  "Day 5: N testers. Here's what broke and what I fixed." Honesty IS
  the content.
- **Day 6 — rest / engagement day.** Reply to every comment across
  all three accounts. Story-only: repost testers' share cards (ask
  first).

## Week 2 — the coach + challenge arc

- **Day 7 [BOL]** DROP 01 recap or launch (whichever timing hits):
  "I dropped a 30-day challenge with one rule: show up." Board
  screen, real member count, join code on screen.
- **Day 8 [LD]** Form Check clip: record a set with the skeleton
  overlay, share the clip natively. Caption: "it frames and counts —
  it doesn't pretend to be your physio. Honest tools only." (This is
  the most shareable visual in the app.)
- **Day 9 [BOL]** Coach-wedge story: film setting up a managed
  client in 60s. "Coaches: your client's profile is ready before
  they ever download. First ten coaches train free for 90 days." CTA
  → DM.
- **Day 10 [HB]** Minimum Win day: real low-energy day, activate the
  fallback on camera. "The app shrank my day instead of guilting me.
  Small still counts." (Ties to the comeback audience.)
- **Day 11 [LD]** PR poster day: chase a real PR; if it lands, the
  gold PR card is the post. If it doesn't, THAT's the post — "no PR
  today, the app doesn't care, the streak does." Either outcome is
  on-brand.
- **Day 12 [BOL]** Tester spotlight: one real tester's week (their
  consent + their card). "N strangers train with this now. Here's
  what one of them did."
- **Day 13 [BOL]** Week-2 numbers, honest: installs, activation,
  what churned people said, what ships next. End the arc where it
  started: "TRAIN HONEST. Link in bio."
