# The gym-floor coach pitch (2026-07-30)

In-person script for the 10-coach pilot. Not a DM template — this is
said out loud, phone in hand, between their sessions. The kit
(docs/COACH-KIT.md) is the follow-up; this is the open.

## The 30-second version (memorize this one)

> "You know how half your clients ghost their programs the week you
> stop watching? I built an app where you set up their profile, they
> claim it with a code, and you see every real session they log —
> with their consent, live. No spreadsheets, no screenshots. I'm
> taking ten coaches into a free 90-day pilot before it costs
> anything. Want to be one of them? Takes five minutes to set up your
> first client — I'll do it with you right now."

Why it works: opens on THEIR pain (client ghosting), names the two
mechanics that matter (claim code, consented live progress), makes
the scarcity honest (ten pilot slots, actually true), and closes on
an action that happens on the spot, not "I'll send you a link."

## The demo (60 seconds, on your phone)

1. Coach dashboard → "this is your roster."
2. Create a managed client with THEIR real client's first name →
   "you just set up a client. Here's the claim code."
3. Your own Progress tab → "this is what they see — and what you see
   the moment they consent. Every number comes from a logged set.
   The app can't fake a streak; that's the whole point."
4. Post-workout share card → "your clients post these. Your name
   rides along as the coach."

Never demo: the AI assistant (it's a utility, not the pitch), the
feed (empty at n=small), anything you have to explain for more than
one sentence.

## The three objections you will actually hear

**"I already use Trainerize / TrueCoach / spreadsheets."**
> "Keep it if it's working. The difference: my pilot is free for 90
> days, your clients' numbers can't be faked, and when a client logs
> a session you see it that minute — not when they remember to fill
> in the sheet. Bring your two flakiest clients and compare."

**"My clients won't use another app."**
> "That's the bet the pilot tests — for free. You set them up, they
> type one code, their history's already inside. If your clients
> don't stick, you lost nothing and told me why."

**"What's it cost after?"**
> "Pilot's free for 90 days. After that it's [PRICE]/month for
> unlimited clients — less than one session's revenue. You'll know
> by then whether it earns it. Pilot coaches keep a locked-in rate."

## The ask ladder (never leave with nothing)

1. Best: set up their first managed client on the spot, hand over
   the claim code.
2. Good: they install from [TESTFLIGHT LINK] while you watch.
3. Fallback: their number, and you text the kit's install blurb
   (COACH-KIT.md §athlete-invite) within the hour — same day, not
   "this week."

## Targets + honesty rules

- 10 pilot coaches in 3 weeks. Track in a note: name, gym, date,
  clients onboarded, follow-up date.
- One follow-up per coach per week, always with THEIR data ("two of
  your three clients logged this week — want the third's code
  reissued?"). Never a generic check-in.
- House rule applies to sales too: no promised features that don't
  exist, no "AI coaching" claims, no invented user counts. "You'd be
  one of the first ten" is the truth and it's a better pitch anyway.

Placeholders: [PRICE] = pricing sign-off (docs/PAYMENTS.md, proposed
$39/mo). [TESTFLIGHT LINK] = the day the $99 clears. Both are
