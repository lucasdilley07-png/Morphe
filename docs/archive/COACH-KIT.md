# Morphe Coach Content Kit

The pilot's ambassador pack. Send this to every coach who joins
(docs/COACH-PILOT.md is the playbook for recruiting them; this is what
they get once they're in). Everything in here follows the house rule:
**every claim is true, every number is logged.** A coach who posts fake
before/afters with our name on it is off the pilot — that's the
zero-tolerance clause working as marketing.

Placeholders: `[PRICE]` waits on the pricing sign-off (docs/PAYMENTS.md);
`[TESTFLIGHT/STORE LINK]` waits on the $99 account. Fill both before the
first kit goes out.

## 1. What you get as a pilot coach (the recap they keep)

- Build every client's profile **before they install anything**, log
  their sessions yourself, and hand them a claim code — when they join,
  **their whole training history is already waiting** in their account.
- When a client consents ("Share with coach"), your dashboard shows
  their **real** streak, weekly volume, sessions, and PRs — live. If
  they toggle it off, it's gone. Consent is theirs, always.
- Your clients get the app free: full logging, programs, rest timers,
  Form Check, recovery check-ins — nothing they need is paywalled.
- Pilot terms: first month free, then [PRICE]/month, unlimited clients.
  You bill your coaching outside Morphe like you do today — we never
  sit between you and your clients' money.

## 3. Onboarding your existing clients — copy-paste scripts

**Text/DM to a current client** (the claim-code moment):

> Moving our training to one app — Morphe. I already set up your
> profile, [NAME], and your last few sessions are in it. Install it,
> enter code **[CODE]** during signup, and everything's waiting.
> Free for you. [TESTFLIGHT/STORE LINK]

**Follow-up if they haven't claimed after a few days:**

> Your Morphe profile's still waiting — 2 minutes: install, enter
> [CODE], done. Your history from our sessions is already in there.

**The consent ask (send AFTER they've logged a session or two):**

> One toggle worth flipping: Profile › "Share with coach." I'll see
> your streak and lifts live, so I can adjust your plan between
> sessions. You can turn it off any time and I see nothing.

Why this order works: history-import gives them a full app on day 0
(not an empty one), and asking for consent after they've trained frames
it as coaching value, not surveillance.

## 5. Posting client results — the rules

1. **Ask first, every time.** Their card, their consent, screenshot the
   yes if you're unsure.
2. **Cards only state logged facts** — never edit a number into a
   screenshot. If it wasn't logged, it doesn't get posted.
3. **No transformation claims with timelines** ("12 weeks to this") —
   post the logged work (sessions, PRs, streaks), not promises.
4. Tag @[MORPHE HANDLE] + #TrainHonest so we can amplify you.

## 6. Objection answers (for DMs and comments)

- **"Another app? My clients hate switching."** — They start with their
  history already in it, not an empty screen. The claim code moves
  everything you've logged for them in one step.
- **"Is my clients' data safe?"** — No ads, no third-party trackers,
  nothing sold — the app's own privacy label says exactly that. Clients
  can export everything as one file or delete their account in-app.
- **"Can I see their workouts without asking?"** — No, and that's the
  point. They flip "Share with coach" themselves; you see live progress
  only while they consent. Sell that TO clients — it's why they trust it.
- **"What's it cost my clients?"** — Nothing. Tracking, programs, Form
  Check, safety features: free. You pay [PRICE]/mo for the coach tools;
  first month free on the pilot.
- **"Does it do video coaching / food tracking?"** — Honest answer: not
  yet. It does workouts, programs, recovery check-ins, form check
  (on-device camera), messaging, and live buddy sessions. We'd rather
  tell you what's real than sell you a roadmap.

## 7. Brand do / don't (one glance)

**Do:** black + volt yellow, the built-in share cards, real screen
recordings, "TRAIN HONEST," momentum-over-perfection language, your own
voice.
**Don't:** fake numbers or staged screenshots, "AI-powered
transformation" claims, medical/injury promises, editing the cards,
bashing named competitors (say "spreadsheets," not brand names).
