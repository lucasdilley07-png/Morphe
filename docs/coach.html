Creator Coach

Coach the people who are just starting.

A Creator Coach account is a free account type for coaches, trainers and experienced lifters who want to put their work in front of Morphe's members. It is granted by application. A person reads every one.

What you can do with it

  • Publish workouts to Discover. Any member can start or save them. Your name is on every one.
  • Write coach notes for Learn: technique points, programming ideas, reminders.
  • Open challenges to everyone. Members join from their board with no code; scores come only from logged workouts.
  • More creator tools arrive first for Creator Coaches.

Who gets accepted

  • Coaches and trainers with a recognised certification, competitive athletes, or lifters with a real track record you can point to.
  • People who will publish honest, safe, beginner-aware work. Morphe does not run medical or rehab advice.
  • You need a Morphe account first. Sign in with it below; the application is tied to that account.

Sign in with your Morphe account

Same email and password as the app. Your password goes to Morphe's sign-in service only, never to this page's host.

Your application

By sending it you agree that what you publish as a Creator Coach carries your name, follows the privacy policy and Morphe's content rules, and can be removed if it doesn't. Decisions show in your Morphe profile. Declined applications can be sent again after you've added to them.
